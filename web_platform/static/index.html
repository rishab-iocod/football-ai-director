1. Camera Scoring Formula

Every frame, every camera receives an autonomous broadcast score:

  • Ball Detected: +100 points when direct vision detects the football.
  • Ball Extrapolated: +60 points when the Physics Kalman filter bridges an occlusion.
  • Center Sweet-Spot: +0 to +30 points based on how well the ball is framed near the middle.
  • Player Density: +1 point per visible player (up to +10).

2. Ball, Goal & Foul Detection

  • High-Recall Ball Tracking: Bridges 1–12 missed frames using 6D physical Kalman filter with drag & gravity.
  • Goal Detection: Triggers when the ball crosses the goal line with high inward velocity, followed by teammate celebration clustering.
  • Foul / Tackle Detection: Detects opposing players colliding at high velocity with sudden aspect-ratio collapse (player grounded/tackled).
  • PROGRAM MONITOR: The main on-air output with dynamic PTZ framing, RoI Zoom PiP, and lower-third graphics.
  • MULTIVIEW SWITCHER: Live tiles for Cam 1, 2, 3, Ball Focus, and Hero Player. The on-air camera is outlined in RED.
  • PAN & ZOOM CONSOLE: Control magnification ($1.0\times - 3.2\times$), switch focus modes, or click-and-drag to pan the camera live.
  • 2D PITCH RADAR: Top-down bird's-eye field showing Home (cyan), Away (red), and moving Ball (gold).
